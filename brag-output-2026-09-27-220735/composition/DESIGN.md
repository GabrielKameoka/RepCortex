# RepCortex Video Design

## Style Prompt
Dark technical product film based directly on the RepCortex dashboard: charcoal navy canvas, violet control accents, green/yellow/red sentiment states, crisp system typography, dense but calm panels, and restrained realtime glow. The product should feel like a focused engineering workspace, not a generic marketing site.

## Colors
- `#090d16` — canvas
- `#111827` — surface cards
- `#7c3aed` — primary accent
- `#f9fafb` — text
- `#10b981` / `#f59e0b` / `#ef4444` — sentiment states

## Typography
- System sans for headings and UI
- SFMono-Regular / ui-monospace for endpoints and architecture labels

## What NOT to Do
- Do not show real API keys, tokens, customer names, or private data.
- Do not use generic blue SaaS gradients.
- Do not use waveform/equalizer visuals.
- Do not make the dashboard look like a fake consumer app.
- Do not flash text faster than it can be read.
